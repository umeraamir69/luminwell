The role

What ambassadors do on Luminwell

You meet students where they are — through messaging and booked sessions — using active listening, validation, and practical signposting. You help them feel less alone and more equipped to take the next step, whether that is campus services, GP support, or crisis resources when needed.

You are never expected to diagnose, treat, or hold clinical risk alone. Welfare pathways and your coordinator exist so difficult conversations are shared responsibly.

Expectations

What we ask of you

Clarity keeps students safe and ambassadors sustainable. These are the non-negotiables we build training around.

Confidentiality & duty of care

Protect student privacy. Escalate through Luminwell when someone may be at risk — never promise secrecy you cannot keep.

Reliability within your availability

Honour the slots you offer and communicate early if study or life means you need to step back temporarily.

Documentation & reflection

Light session notes help coordinators support you and maintain continuity for students — we will show you how.

For coordinators

Running a safe ambassador programme

Staff use the admin flows to vet applicants, complete onboarding checklists, and add profiles when you are provisioning accounts directly. Pair this page with your institution’s safeguarding policy — Luminwell is the workflow layer, not a replacement for local governance.

Questions about eligibility or capacity? Route them through your Luminwell programme lead before inviting new ambassadors onto the platform.
