What are some mindfulness exercises I can try when I feel overwhelmed?
9:28 AM
L

Here are three evidence-based techniques that work well when you're overwhelmed. They're quick, private, and you don't need any equipment.

🫁

Box breathing (4–4–4–4)

Inhale 4 counts · Hold 4 · Exhale 4 · Hold 4. Repeat 3–4 times. Activates your parasympathetic nervous system within seconds.

🖐

5-4-3-2-1 grounding

Name 5 things you see · 4 you can touch · 3 you hear · 2 you smell · 1 you taste. Pulls attention into the present moment.

🧘

Body scan (2 minutes)

Close your eyes. Slowly notice sensations from your feet up to your head without trying to change anything. Reduces physical tension.
