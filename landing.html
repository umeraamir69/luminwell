What is Luminwell?

Peer support, clearly explained

Luminwell connects you with trained peer ambassadors — people who have lived through similar challenges and are certified to offer structured emotional support. It’s not therapy, and that’s entirely intentional.

Peer support is built on shared human experience. Your ambassador isn’t a licensed therapist, but they’re trained, supervised, and genuinely committed to walking alongside you at your own pace — with no wait lists.

Your privacy, always

Built for trust from the ground up

When you share something deeply personal, security isn’t optional. Every layer of Luminwell is designed to protect you.

Password-protected journals

Your journal is encrypted end-to-end and locked behind a password or biometric. Nobody — not even Luminwell — can read it.

Encrypted conversations

All chats are end-to-end encrypted. Session notes are identity-protected. Your conversations stay between you and your ambassador.

No third-party authentication

We don’t use Google or Facebook login. Biometric and passcode options are available — secure, simple, no third-party tracking.

Transparent data practices

We explain exactly what data is collected, how it’s stored, and why. Export or delete your data from Settings at any time.
