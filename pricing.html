Questions

Straight answers

Pricing should be predictable. Here is how we think about it.

What happens if I stay on Free?

You keep core access: onboarding, matching, a limited number of chat sessions each month, basic check-ins, and a small journal — plus crisis resources. Premium unlocks depth and frequency.

Do I need a card to try Premium?

Trial and billing details are confirmed at signup. Crisis support is never paywalled: 988 and help links stay visible everywhere in the app.

Institutions & groups

Universities and partners sometimes sponsor seats. Ask your wellbeing office if Luminwell is included in your campus offer.
